AI Fluency — Week 2

How to Use This Tool

For each task, score five factors from 1 (Low) to 3 (High). The total score tells you whether to delegate fully to AI, delegate partially with human review, or keep the task human-led.

Scoring Guide

5 – 8
Delegate Fully
AI can handle this independently. Little risk.
9 – 11
Delegate Partially
AI can assist, but a human should review.
12 – 15
Keep Human-Led
Too complex, high-stakes, or sensitive for AI alone.
